About Me

From Nepal to New Mexico: A journey in applied geography

I am a graduate researcher in Applied Geography at New Mexico State University, specializing in remote sensing, GIScience, and ArcGIS Indoors and geospatial data analysis. My work focuses on vegetation dynamics, land-use change, and climate–landscape interactions in arid and semi-arid regions using satellite time-series data, geospatial modeling, and spatial databases. I am particularly interested in GeoAI and deep learning approaches for advancing environmental analysis and smart space planning.

Academic Journey

From Geomatics Engineering to Applied Geography

My academic journey began in Nepal, where I earned a B.E. in Geomatics Engineering from Tribhuvan University. During my undergraduate studies, I witnessed firsthand how natural hazards like floods and landslides impact communities, sparking my interest in using geospatial technologies for environmental monitoring and disaster risk reduction.

Research Focus: I bridge Earth observation data with practical applications, focusing on vegetation phenology in Sky Islands, urban growth impacts in the Rio Grande corridor, and developing GIS tools for better environmental management.

Currently pursuing my M.S. in Applied Geography at NMSU, I'm investigating vegetation phenology dynamics across the Sonoran, Chihuahuan, and Mojave Deserts using MODIS and Landsat time-series data under Dr. Michaela Buenemann's guidance.

My Research Approach Builds on Three Pillars:

1

Earth Observation & Remote Sensing

I use satellite data from MODIS, Landsat, and Sentinel platforms to analyze environmental change across space and time. My work focuses on scalable time-series analytics for vegetation dynamics and surface processes, using cloud-based platforms and automated workflows to support monitoring, trend detection, and decision-ready insights.

Sky Islands Vegetation Phenology

Investigating how mountain "islands" in desert regions create unique microclimates that support distinct vegetation patterns. Using Phenofit, BFAST, and Google Earth Engine to analyze phenological metrics across desert transitions.

2

Spatial Analytics & GIS Solutions

I design and implement applied GIS solutions that translate spatial data into actionable outputs. My work includes building ArcGIS Indoors information models, spatial databases, and interactive dashboards, as well as developing spatial analysis workflows for urban growth, land-use change, and environmental planning. These efforts bridge research, operations, and decision-making. Project Details.

3

Applied Intelligence & Data Analysis

I am interested in integrating data analytics, GeoAI, and deep learning techniques with geospatial workflows to enhance spatial intelligence. This includes automating analysis, identifying patterns in large spatial datasets, and developing scalable approaches for environmental monitoring, smart space planning, and data-driven decision support.
